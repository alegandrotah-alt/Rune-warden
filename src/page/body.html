RUNE WARDEN

Raise heroes. Twist the road. Hold the keep.

How to play
The road
Monsters come out of the portal on the left and walk to your keep on the right. Every hero you place blocks a tile, so the heroes are the maze. Twist the road to make monsters walk past your heroes for longer. You can never block it completely.
The shop
Tap a hero card to buy it to your bench, then drag it onto the field between waves. Drag a hero onto the shop to sell it. Gold earns interest: 1 gold for every 10 you hold, up to 5. Win and loss streaks pay extra.
Stars
Three copies of the same hero merge into a ★2, and three ★2 into a ★3. Each star is much stronger.
Levels
Your level is how many heroes you can field. Buy XP to level faster; higher levels find rarer heroes in the shop.
Traits
Every hero has an origin (Kingdom, Wildwood, Shadow) and a class (Guardian, Marksman, Sorcerer, Assassin). Field different heroes that share a trait to switch on its bonus, shown on the left.
× Runes
Arrows and spells that fly over a blue × rune are multiplied, once per rune. Place ranged heroes so their shots cross runes. Rune stones you earn can be dropped on any empty tile.
× Portals
A monster that walks through a purple × portal splits into that many copies, and every copy drops gold when it dies. Route monsters through portals for profit, or around them to stay safe.
Spells
Heroes fill their blue mana bar by attacking. When it is full they cast their special ability.
Relics
After some waves and every boss you choose a relic that lasts the whole run. Three acts, three bosses. Win, then try endless mode.
Keys
Space: fight or change speed. D: reroll. F: buy XP. P: pause.
