    RUNE WARDEN

    Raise heroes. Twist the road. Hold the keep.

    Normal
      How to play
      The road
      Monsters come out of the portal and walk to your keep (left to right with the phone sideways, top to bottom with it upright); later floors open more gates along the sides and breaches in the middle of the field, and a glowing gate shows how many monsters it will send. The field is a board of hexes; every hero you place stands on a raised stone plinth and blocks that hex, so the heroes are the maze. Twist the road to make monsters walk past your heroes for longer. You can never block a gate's road completely.
      Frenzy
      If a fight drags on past two minutes the remaining monsters go into a frenzy: they run faster and shrug off knockback, stuns, sleep and charm.
      Zoom
      Use the − and + buttons at the top, pinch, or double-tap the field to zoom; drag an empty spot to look around while zoomed in. The game remembers your zoom.
      Terrain
      High ground gives shooters +1 range and melee heroes +20% damage. Mud slows monsters by 35%, thorns make them bleed, and nothing can walk or stand on water (shots fly over it). Tap a hex to see what it is.
      Biomes
      Each act rolls one of two biomes: the Hollow Crypts or the Sunken Bog, the Wildwood Marches or Frostpeak Pass, the Ember Wastes or the Sunscorch Dunes. Each has its own monsters, terrain and boss.
      The shop
      Tap a hero card to see what it does, its traits and abilities; tap it again or press Buy to send it to your bench, then drag it onto the field between waves. Drag a hero onto the shop to sell it. Gold earns interest: 1 gold for every 10 you hold, up to 5. Win and loss streaks pay extra.
      Stars
      Three copies of the same hero merge into a ★2, and three ★2 into a ★3. Each star is much stronger.
      Levels
      Your level is how many heroes you can field. Buy XP to level faster; higher levels find rarer heroes in the shop.
      Traits
      Every hero has an origin (Kingdom, Wildwood, Shadow, Plaguebringer, Beast) and a class (Guardian, Marksman, Sorcerer, Assassin, Bruiser). Beasts grow stronger next to other Beasts; Bruisers knock monsters back up the road. Field different heroes that share a trait to switch on its bonus, shown in the trait list.
      × Runes
      Arrows and spells that fly over a blue × rune are multiplied, once per rune. Place ranged heroes so their shots cross runes. Rune stones you earn can be dropped on any empty tile.
      × Portals
      A monster that walks through a purple × portal splits into that many copies, and every copy drops gold when it dies. Route monsters through portals for profit, or around them to stay safe.
      Spells
      Heroes fill their blue mana bar by attacking. When it is full they cast their special ability.
      Health
      Monsters swing at the heroes they walk past, and some throw bolts or slam the ground. A hero whose green health bar runs out is knocked out until the wave ends, but still blocks the road. Guardians are tougher and monsters prefer to hit them.
      Roles
      ⚔ Frontline heroes fight up close: they have 35% more health, slow monsters next to them, and most have an aura or a control effect (sleep, stun, taunt, faster or tougher neighbours). ➶ Backline heroes do the damage from range. Drag a hero to see the hexes it reaches; road hexes in reach glow brighter. Legends (5-cost heroes) each have a unique power.
      Monsters
      Hunters stop to fight any hero in reach. Runners ignore your heroes and rush the keep. Wraiths phase out of reach now and then. Elite champions carry a twist (two in the Rift and in endless): faster, shielded, splitting, molten, warding their pack, unstoppable and more. Tap a monster to see what it does.
      The keep
      Keep guardians fight from the walls: archers, a trebuchet that reaches the whole field, a priestess who heals, a chronomancer who stops time, and more. Unlock them in the Keep tab through deeds or gems and train them up to level 5. Tap the keep during a run to see them.
      The Rune Devourer
      Beat the Act III boss and the Rift opens; fight through it and the Devourer waits at the bottom. It loses at most 1.6% of its health a second and regrows it, ignores most crowd control, sheds an immune shell at every quarter, seizes your strongest hero, eats your runes, tears breaches in the field, and its Beat of Death grows deadlier with every beat. You need a long road, healers and steady damage. Your victory already counts; this fight is for glory.
      Items
      Items appear in the ITEMS rack by your bench. Tap an item to read it; tap a hero to see what its equipped items do. Drag one onto a hero. Two components on the same hero forge a finished item, and a hero holds up to three. Tap an item to read it and see its recipes. Selling a hero gives its items back.
      The map
      Each act is a map of branching roads ending at the boss. Before every floor pick a room joined to where you stand. Battles and Elites send a wave; Elites bring champions with a twist and give an item, a relic and gold. Campfires (rest or train), the Merchant, Treasure chests and ? rooms have no monsters; they pay base income but no interest, while every fight pays bonus gold. After each act boss the keep repairs half of its missing health, and rune stones you placed come with you into the next act.
      Recruits
      After every fight, choose one of three heroes to join you for free, or skip for 2 gold.
      Veterans
      Train a hero at a campfire or the merchant: each veteran rank gives +25% damage and health (two ranks at most). Veterans show chevrons on their plinth.
      Potions
      You carry up to two potions, shown at the top of the screen. Tap one to drink it; most work during a wave.
      Relics
      Relics come from elites, chests, the merchant, events and bosses. The ◈ button lists yours. Boss relics are strong and most have a price.
      Poison
      Plaguebringer heroes stack poison on monsters. Each stack eats a share of the hero's damage every second, up to 12 stacks.
      Ascension and dailies
      Win a run to unlock the next ascension level: harder rules, more gems. The daily challenge gives everyone the same shops, waves and map for the day.
      Acts
      Three acts of ten floors, three bosses. Win, then go down into the Rift (act IV: five floors of void-born monsters and the Rift Herald) to reach the Rune Devourer, or try endless mode.
      Wardens
      Choose who you play on the Play tab. Each warden has a perk, a favoured keep guardian that fights one level higher when stationed, and a power you can use once per fight with the button at the top of the screen. New wardens unlock as you beat act bosses and win runs.
      Run summaries
      The Records tab and the end screen have a Copy button that puts a plain-text summary of your runs on the clipboard: how far you got, what ended the run, your team, traits and relics.
      Keys
      Space: fight or change speed. D: reroll. F: buy XP. Q: warden power. P: pause.
